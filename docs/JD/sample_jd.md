# VP of Engineering – Cloud Platform

**Company:** Acme Corp  
**Location:** Remote / Dallas, TX  

## About the Role

We are looking for a Vice President of Engineering to lead our Cloud Platform organization. You will own the architecture and delivery of a multi-cloud infrastructure serving 10M+ users globally, drive a FinOps culture across engineering, and scale a team of 80+ engineers.

## Responsibilities

- Define and execute the cloud platform strategy across AWS and Azure
- Lead site reliability engineering (SRE) programs targeting 99.99% availability
- Drive DevSecOps transformation and shift-left security practices
- Build and mentor a world-class engineering organization
- Partner with the CTO and product leadership on technology roadmap and investment planning
- Oversee FinOps governance and cloud cost optimization
- Lead vendor negotiations and strategic procurement
- Provide technical due diligence for M&A activities

## Requirements

- 15+ years of technology leadership experience
- Proven track record delivering enterprise cloud infrastructure at scale
- Deep expertise in AWS (EKS, Lambda, Bedrock, RDS) and Azure (AKS, Functions)
- Experience with Kubernetes, Terraform, and Infrastructure as Code (IaC)
- Strong background in AI/ML platform engineering and GenAI adoption
- Experience with LLM frameworks (LangChain, RAG pipelines, agentic workflows)
- History of managing large engineering budgets ($20M+)
- Executive communication skills; experience advising C-suite
